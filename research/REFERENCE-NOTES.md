# Reference sites, what we take from each (scraped 2026-10-16)

Raw material (rendered HTML, stylesheets, computed type scale, full-page screenshots) lives in `research/reference-sites/<site>/` (gitignored, regenerate with `node scripts/scrape-refs.mjs`). This file holds the conclusions.

## Shared patterns

- Light, slightly textured canvas (Dapper #f3f3f3 with grain, Workflows #F4F6F7 with dot haze, Origami #fafafa, Anthropic ivory #faf9f5). White cards with 16 to 24px radius on top of it. Dark used once: the footer (Dennis, Anthropic) or a single feature band (Workflows, Apple).
- One display face, set huge and tight: Dapper h1 ~90px Helvetica Now Display 700 at -0.02em, Dylan 274px condensed, Dennis 216px, Origami 88px serif, Anthropic 61px sans plus 80px serif. Everything else small (12 to 15px labels, 15 to 17px body).
- A second voice for emphasis: italic serif words inside sans headlines (Dapper Ivy Presto, Origami Frank Ruhl Libre, Anthropic Serif), mono for labels (Dylan IBM Plex Mono, Anthropic JetBrains Mono).
- One accent, used as a small object: Dapper green square arrow boxes (#74f5a1), Workflows lime button (#E0FC04), Dennis blue circle (#455CE9). Never as a background.
- Hero = left-aligned statement, object on the right (Dapper plant, Dylan 3D monitor, Apple product), intro paragraph bottom-left, one overlapping proof card bottom-right (Dapper "54% increase", Workflows featured case).
- Proof as numbers in cards: logo plus two metric tiles (Dapper), featured case plus three metrics (Workflows), four stat columns (Origami).
- Motion: Lenis smooth scroll, GSAP reveals, horizontal carousels with arrow buttons, marquee strips (Dylan skewed logo band), magnetic round buttons (Dennis), scroll-bound 3D object (Dapper Spline plant).
- Footer as a destination: Dennis "Let's work together" with a round blue "Get in touch" and contact pills; Dapper rounded dark top edge; Anthropic dark four-column footer.

## dapper.agency, deep scrape 17-10 (scripts/scrape-dapper-deep.mjs, research/reference-sites/dapper/deep/)

- **Pixel grid.** `.pixel-grid` clusters of 3em `.pixel-grid-pixel` squares in the brand green, scattered next to headlines, under the hero and around the results header. GSAP sets a random opacity per square on a loop (twinkle), ScrollTrigger resets them on enter/leave, and classes `is--move-y-200` / `is--move-x-200` shift single squares by two cells while scrolling so the cluster re-arranges itself. Ours: `.pixels` with `--cols`, `data-rows`, `data-lit`; cells are built in `site.js`, twinkle with GSAP, drift per cell on a scrubbed ScrollTrigger over the section, hidden to -1 inside an isolated section so content stays on top.
- **Results.** Bold headline left (two lines, no italic now), paragraph plus button plus prev/next arrows right, then a horizontal rail of white cards 22.5em high: client logo top-left, arrow box top-right, two metric tiles at the bottom, and a progress counter "01 / 14" next to the arrows. Ours: the v4 `.results` rail with `data-carousel-progress`.
- **Expertise storytelling.** Two big white cards, each with a bold lead sentence, two paragraphs, a "The result?" checklist with green check squares and a "Discover more" link, with a sticky visual beside the card while you scroll. Ours: `.expertise` with `.exp__visual` (sticky at 110px) and `.exp__card`; visuals are a verified ICP list and a reply thread that ends in a booked meeting.
- **Quotes slider.** White cards with a thin left bar, a large light quote, a round headshot with name and title bottom-left and the client logo bottom-right, arrows and counter in the section head. Ours: `.quotes__track` with `.qcard`.
- **Cases page.** Big cards with a photo and metric tiles overlapping the photo, progress "01 of 14" under the rail. Not built yet, candidate for the case-studies page roll-out.
- Dropped on purpose: Dapper's italic serif words inside headlines (Max 17-10: no italics anywhere) and the green as a surface; the accent stays light blue `#72C1FF` as small objects only.

## Video-hosting voor VSL en testimonials, onderzoek 17-10

Wat de referentiesites doen (uit de eigen scrapes): Dapper = Vimeo (lightbox plus achtergrond-loops), Dylan Brouwer = Bunny Stream (HLS) plus Vimeo, Dennis Snellenberg = zelf gehoste korte mp4-loops zonder geluid, workflows.io = YouTube-embeds, Anthropic = YouTube (nocookie), Apple = zelf gehoste mp4. Patroon: een klein stil loopje in de kaart, de volledige video via een streaming-host in een lightbox.

Materiaal: vijf testimonial-cuts van 5:16 tot 6:48 (samen ~29 min, 83 tot 142 MB per stuk op 1080p) plus de VSL van 3:34 op Wistia. Te lang en te zwaar om als losse mp4 in de repo te zetten; streaming met adaptieve kwaliteit is nodig.

Prijzen (officiële pagina's, 17-10-2026): Wistia Free 25 GB opslag, 200 GB bandbreedte, 1 gebruiker, Wistia-branding; Business $79/mnd (jaar) zonder branding; geen Wistia-leverancier in Moneybird, dus vermoedelijk het gratis plan. Mux pay-as-you-go $0/mnd met $20 krediet per maand, 100.000 afgeleverde minuten gratis, opslag $0,003 per minuut per maand (32 min = ~$0,10), witlabel Mux Player, VS. Bunny Stream opslag $0,01/GB (Frankfurt), levering $0,01/GB EU/NA, encoding gratis, minimum $1/mnd, EU-bedrijf, witlabel speler. Cloudflare Stream $5 per 1.000 min opslag (vooruitbetaald per blok) en $1 per 1.000 min levering, ~$5 tot 6/mnd. Vimeo Standard ~$20/mnd zonder branding (derde bron, Vimeo's pagina rendert alleen met JS). YouTube gratis maar zware speler, cookies en aanbevolen video's.

Advies: Bunny Stream (EU, ~1 dollar per maand, upload via dashboard, witlabel) of Mux (gratis op dit volume, mooiste speler, VS). Wistia alleen houden als het logo op de speler geen bezwaar is of als de $79/mnd voor analytics gewenst is. Ongeacht de host: stille teaser-loops van 6 seconden in de kaarten, volledige video in de lightbox.
