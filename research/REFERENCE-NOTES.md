# Reference sites, what we take from each (scraped 2026-10-16)

Raw material (rendered HTML, stylesheets, computed type scale, full-page screenshots) lives in `research/reference-sites/<site>/` (gitignored, regenerate with `node scripts/scrape-refs.mjs`). This file holds the conclusions.

## Shared patterns

- Light, slightly textured canvas (Dapper #f3f3f3 with grain, Workflows #F4F6F7 with dot haze, Origami #fafafa, Anthropic ivory #faf9f5). White cards with 16 to 24px radius on top of it. Dark used once: the footer (Dennis, Anthropic) or a single feature band (Workflows, Apple).
- One display face, set huge and tight: Dapper h1 ~90px Helvetica Now Display 700 at -0.02em, Dylan 274px condensed, Dennis 216px, Origami 88px serif, Anthropic 61px sans plus 80px serif. Everything else small (12 to 15px labels, 15 to 17px body).
- A second voice for emphasis: italic serif words inside sans headlines (Dapper Ivy Presto, Origami Frank Ruhl Libre, Anthropic Serif), mono for labels (Dylan IBM Plex Mono, Anthropic JetBrains Mono).
- One accent, used as a small object: Dapper green square arrow boxes (#74f5a1), Workflows lime button (#E0FC04), Dennis blue circle (#455CE9). Never as a background.
- Hero = left-aligned statement, object on the right (Dapper plant, Dylan 3D monitor, Apple product), intro paragraph bottom-left, one overlapping proof card bottom-right (Dapper "54% increase", Workflows featured case).
- Proof as numbers in cards: logo plus two metric tiles (Dapper), featured case plus three metrics (Workflows), four stat columns (Origami).
- Motion: Lenis smooth scroll, GSAP reveals, horizontal carousels with arrow buttons, marquee strips (Dylan skewed logo band), magnetic round buttons (Dennis), scroll-bound 3D object (Dapper Spline plant).
- Footer as a destination: Dennis "Let's work together" with a round blue "Get in touch" and contact pills; Dapper rounded dark top edge; Anthropic dark four-column footer.

## workflows.io deep scrape (2026-10-17), the blueprint for v3

Max's call: replicate workflows.io as closely as possible first, then adjust copy and styling. Deep scrape in `research/reference-sites/workflows/deep/` (regenerate with `node scripts/scrape-workflows-deep.mjs`): `cssom.css` (all 178 KB of rules), `outline.json` (DOM with computed styles), `probe.json` (hero, nav, pill and form styles), 16 viewport screenshots, hover captures.

What the page is made of, top to bottom: fixed particle canvas with mouse parallax; light dome hero (#ECF0F2 to #F4F6F7) with eyebrow pill, 56px two-tone headline (weights 270 and 400), 20px sub, Name + Email + lime "Request Call" rail, chips, "Watch video", trusted-by avatars; customers (featured case card + 4x4 logo discs); services headline + one big card with 4 cells (tag pills, 30px light titles, "Learn more" with square arrow box, a UI visual per cell); dark "LEARN" band with three tilted screens; dark problem/solution band with sticky card and a glowing vertical flow of nodes; "WHY US" comparison table with the brand column raised; dark CTA grid with form; partners rail with dotted top line; case carousel with scaled centre card; three-column testimonials with fact tiles; two-column FAQ with plus icons and a raised open item; dark footer with star dome, headline with inline logo, form, footer card and badges.

Tokens: Helvetica Neue stack (we use Inter), weights 270/400/500, sizes 3.5rem, 3rem, 1.875rem, 1.25rem, 1rem, .875rem, .75rem; radii .5rem, .75rem, 1rem, 1.375rem, 1.5rem, 6.25rem; neumorphic shadows (card: 0 0 1rem rgba(0,0,0,.04) plus inset .5rem light and dark), pill border gradient 135deg #fff to #dadfe1, eases cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1), header theme transition .38s; lime #E0FC04 (ours: light blue #72C1FF), text #0C0D0E and #141801, greys #3C4344 #6b787a #8f8f90, dark #020304.

Interactions: nav collapses into a three-pill group after scroll and flips to dark over dark sections; "Request Call" grows a black circle from the cursor (550ms cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)) and the text turns lime; "Learn more" has a two-span sliding underline; cards lift 2px on hover with a brighter inset shadow.

## v4 (2026-10-17), Dapper composition on top, workflows.io interaction layer, vault and engine kept

Max's call after v3: the dark vault and the engine are right, everything above them was not; start with the VSL, put the video testimonials straight after it, take the Dapper landing page as the model (not a copy, a mix of a few sites), and make it far more interactive ("crazy visuals on hover, a billion-dollar feel"; the pills need a light that travels around them, not a hover).

What v4 takes from Dapper: the hero as a left-aligned three-line statement (Inter 500, -0.028em, 80px) with an italic serif word (Instrument Serif), eyebrow top-left, the object top-right (our 3D glass mark in the plant's spot, one turn over the hero), intro paragraph bottom-left, and the featured card bottom-right overlapping the hero rule (ours is the VSL card, Wistia lightbox). Section heads split in two (eyebrow and big headline left, paragraph, link and carousel arrows right). Results as logo plus two metric tiles per card, then a client strip. Services as headline-right plus white cards on the canvas, with the v3 visuals inside. Pixel squares as decor. What stays from workflows.io: neumorphic cards and pills, the name/email/Request Call rail, ripple buttons, letter hover, the dark LEARN and engine bands, compare table, FAQ, footer.

Interaction layer (site.css and site.js, fine pointers only where it says so): `.beam` conic border light on eyebrows, tags, chips and the Free badge (always on, faster on hover, `@property --beam`); cursor dot plus trailing ring that grows on links and becomes a "Play" disc over video posters (`[data-cursor="play"]`); magnetic buttons and arrow boxes; `.spot` pointer-following glow on cards (multiply on light, screen on dark); `.tilt` on the VSL and story cards; `[data-words]` word-by-word headline reveal; service visuals animate on card hover (Slack cards rise, calendar bars fill, flow lines run and turn accent, radial rings pulse); vault cards compose their fan transform from CSS variables so the hover lifts a card without flattening the others.

Page order now: hero (VSL), client stories (video carousel, posters from the testimonial recordings, `data-wistia-id` empty until the videos are on Wistia, chip "Video coming soon"), results (six cases as metric cards plus the client strip; the old detailed case carousel below partners is gone), services, dark vault, dark engine (poster in the brand node, the live 3D sits in the hero), why us, dark CTA, partners, text testimonials with fact tiles, FAQ, footer.
