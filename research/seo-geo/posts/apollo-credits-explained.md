## The plans in November 2026

Prices checked on 11 November 2026 on [apollo.io/pricing](https://www.apollo.io/pricing). Prices per seat per month, billed annually, USD. Monthly billing is available for some plans at a higher price.

| Plan | Price per seat per month | Credits per seat per year | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 900 (granted monthly) | 2 sequences, basic filters |
| Basic | $49 | 30,000 (granted upfront) | unlimited sequences, waterfall enrichment, CRM integrations |
| Professional | $79 | 48,000 (granted upfront) | adds AI lead scoring, A/Z testing, more call recording |
| Organization | $119 | 72,000 (granted upfront) | minimum 3 seats, so at least $357 a month |

All seats on one account must be on the same plan. Above Organization there is a custom plan.

## How the credit pool works

- **One pool.** Credits are pooled across the whole team. A three-seat Professional account has 144,000 credits a year to share, not 48,000 per person in separate buckets.
- **Granted upfront.** On paid plans the full year's credits are available from day one.
- **No rollover.** Credits reset at the start of each billing cycle. Unused credits are gone.
- **One currency.** Credits now pay for emails, phone numbers, email warmup, domains and mailboxes, and AI research. Older articles about separate email, mobile and export credits describe an earlier credit model.

## What a credit buys

Apollo's [API pricing docs](https://docs.apollo.io/docs/api-pricing) list these rates. Your account shows the in-app rates under its credit settings; check there before you plan a big run.

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| Email address (charged only if found) | 1 |
| Mobile number | 8 on top of the email |
| Email waterfall | typically 1 to 4 |
| Phone waterfall | typically 8 to 25 |
| Office or HQ number | free |

## What a contact really costs

Divide the plan price by its credits: $588 for 30,000 on Basic, $948 for 48,000 on Professional, $1,428 for 72,000 on Organization. All three land at about $0.02 per credit. So:

- an email: about $0.02
- an email plus mobile number: about 9 credits, roughly $0.18
- 10,000 contacts with email only: about 10,000 credits, a third of one Basic seat's yearly pool

The expensive part is phone numbers. If calling is not your main channel, do not pull mobiles by default.

## How we use Apollo

Apollo is in our stack as a sourcing tool, not as the final word on an email. Its data is strong in the US and weaker in the Netherlands and much of Europe, so we use it to find companies and people and then verify emails in a separate waterfall in [Clay](/blog/clay-pricing-explained). Two habits save credits:

1. **Narrow first, reveal later.** Build and filter the list first, and spend credits on emails only for the people you will actually contact this month.
2. **Plan around the reset.** Credits do not roll over, so spread big enrichment runs over the cycle instead of saving credits for later.

Our full tool list with what we pay is on the [GTM stack page](/resources/gtm-stack). If you want the whole system (sourcing, enrichment, sending and CRM) run for you, see what an [outbound agency](/outbound-agency) does.
