## The plans side by side

Prices checked on 11 November 2026 on [instantly.ai/pricing](https://instantly.ai/pricing) and [smartlead.ai/pricing](https://www.smartlead.ai/pricing). Monthly billing, USD.

| Tool and plan | Price per month | Contacts | Emails per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instantly Growth | $47 | 1,000 uploaded | 5,000 |
| Instantly Hypergrowth | $107 | 25,000 uploaded | 125,000 |
| Instantly Light Speed | $358 | 100,000 uploaded | 500,000 |
| Smartlead Base | $39 | 2,000 | 6,000 |
| Smartlead Pro | $94 | 30,000 | 90,000 |
| Smartlead Unlimited Smart | $174 | unlimited | 150,000 |
| Smartlead Unlimited Prime | $379 | unlimited | 500,000 |

Both offer an annual discount. Smartlead's yearly prices are $32, $78, $144 and $315 a month (17% off). Instantly shows its own yearly toggle on the pricing page. Both have custom enterprise plans above these.

## Cost per 1,000 emails at full use

Divide the plan price by the emails it allows:

| Plan | Cost per 1,000 emails |
|---|---|
| Smartlead Base | $6.50 |
| Instantly Growth | $9.40 |
| Smartlead Pro | $1.04 |
| Instantly Hypergrowth | $0.86 |
| Smartlead Unlimited Smart | $1.16 |
| Instantly Light Speed | $0.72 |
| Smartlead Unlimited Prime | $0.76 |

At small volume Smartlead is cheaper. From roughly 25,000 emails a month Instantly Hypergrowth gives the most room per dollar, and at half a million emails the two top plans are close.

## What the limits actually mean

- **Contacts.** Instantly counts uploaded contacts. Smartlead counts contacts stored in the account (each unique email address is a lead credit). Both cap how big your active audience can be, so check this number before the email number.
- **Email accounts.** Both connect unlimited mailboxes on every plan. Instantly also includes unlimited warmup on every plan.
- **Extras.** Instantly Hypergrowth adds a CRM built into Unibox, its shared reply inbox. Smartlead sells client workspaces as add-ons from Pro up (white label is $29 a month per client workspace) and includes three with Prime.

## The costs that are not on the pricing page

The sending tool is the smaller part of the bill. What you also pay for:

1. **Domains.** Never send cold email from your main domain. We use two to three extra domains per client.
2. **Mailboxes.** Two inboxes per domain on Google Workspace or Microsoft 365. Smartlead resells them from $4.50 per Google or Outlook mailbox per month ($3.99 for SMTP) plus $13 to $19 per domain per year.
3. **Lead data.** Both sell lead credits separately, and most teams enrich elsewhere (we use [Clay](/blog/clay-pricing-explained)).
4. **Time.** Warmup takes weeks before a new domain sends at full volume.

## Which one we use

We run every client on Instantly and are an Instantly Certified Expert, so read this with that bias in mind. For an agency the deciding factor is rarely the price difference, which is small at real volume. It is how cleanly the tool separates clients, how fast you can see which inbox is hurting deliverability, and how well replies flow into the CRM.
