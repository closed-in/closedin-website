FAQs

Frequently Asked
Questions

What does closed:in do?
closed:in is a GTM engineering agency in Amsterdam. We build and run outbound sales systems for B2B companies, combining cold email (Instantly), LinkedIn outreach, lead enrichment (Clay), website visitor tracking and CRM orchestration into one managed engine. Most clients see qualified meetings booked within 2-3 weeks.
How is closed:in different from a traditional cold email agency?
Traditional cold email agencies send volume. closed:in is a GTM engineering agency, meaning we build a full outbound system on top of your CRM. That includes enriched, signal-based lead lists via Clay, multi-channel sequences across email and LinkedIn, deliverability infrastructure, and reply handling wired straight into your sales pipeline.
What kind of companies do you work with?
B2B service companies, SaaS founders, recruitment and PE firms, agencies and product companies selling into mid-market and enterprise. Our clients usually have an average contract value of EUR 5K or more and need a predictable, repeatable way to book qualified meetings without hiring an in-house SDR team.
How fast do you deliver results?
Setup of infrastructure takes 2 to 3 weeks (domains, inboxes, warmup, lists, copy, sequences). Most clients get their first qualified meeting within 2-3 weeks of launch. Documented case studies show 78 to 247 opportunities per engagement, with pipeline values from USD 128K to EUR 1.56M.
Which tools and platforms does closed:in use?
Core stack: Instantly for cold email, Clay for enrichment and orchestration, Close CRM for pipeline management, Apollo and Sales Navigator for sourcing, and LinkedIn for direct outreach. We are a Clay Solutions Partner, an Instantly Certified Expert and an Apollo Partner.
Where is closed:in based and which regions do you serve?
closed:in is based in Amsterdam, Netherlands. We run campaigns worldwide. The Netherlands, Belgium, the United Kingdom, the broader European market and the United States, in English, Dutch or other local languages depending on the target country.
How do I get started?
Book a 30 minute discovery call. We'll review your current outbound setup, your ICP and your goals, and confirm whether closed:in is a fit before sending a tailored proposal.
