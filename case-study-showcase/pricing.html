01Four pricing drivers

Why pricing is a range,
not a fixed number.

Two clients on paper sending the same email volume can require very different work. A SaaS company with a clean Apollo ICP and HubSpot already wired up is a different build than a recruitment firm targeting PE-backed portfolio CFOs across 4 countries with custom Clay sourcing and a Close CRM rebuild. These four drivers decide where you land between EUR 4K and EUR 8K.

01

ICP complexity (Clay)

Standard ICPs that sit cleanly in Apollo or Sales Navigator are at the lower end. Custom ICPs like hospitality, boutique retail, M&A advisory or niche industrials require a custom Clay table with Google Maps scraping, website parsing or Claygent prompts. The deeper the Clay engineering, the higher in the range.

02

Sending volume

More sending volume means more domains, more inboxes and more deliverability monitoring. A standard 3-step campaign at 1,000 emails per day is roughly 7,000 unique prospects per month. Higher volume tiers add infrastructure cost that flows into the monthly fee.

03

Channels (email, LinkedIn, multi-channel)

Cold email only sits at the lower end. Adding LinkedIn outbound (connection requests, DMs, content amplification) or multi-channel sequences with signal-based plays adds engineering and ongoing management. Multi-channel engagements typically sit in the upper half of the range.

04

CRM depth

Light reporting into a Google Sheet is at the lower end. Full pipeline orchestration with reply handling, lead scoring and stage automation wired into Close, HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive adds setup time and ongoing maintenance.

02Three engagement shapes

What EUR 4K, 6K
and 8K actually buy.

These are typical shapes, not fixed plans. Every engagement is quoted after a 30 minute discovery call. The constant across all three: Clay enrichment, full sending infrastructure, weekly reporting and reply handling are included from day one.

EUR 4K/mo

Foundation

Cold email only. Standard ICP that maps cleanly to Apollo or Sales Navigator. Light CRM integration. Best for B2B SaaS or service companies with a defined ICP that want a clean, predictable engine.

Includes: Dedicated sending infrastructure, Clay waterfall enrichment, multi-step sequence, weekly reports, reply handling, Close or HubSpot light sync.

EUR 6K/mo

Growth

Multi-channel: cold email plus LinkedIn outbound. Mid-complexity ICP, may need custom Claygent prompts. Full CRM orchestration. Most closed:in engagements sit here.

Includes Foundation plus: LinkedIn DMs and connection requests, custom Claygent prompts, signal-based scoring, full Close or HubSpot pipeline orchestration, A/B testing.

EUR 8K/mo

Enterprise

Custom ICP that requires bespoke Clay sourcing (Google Maps, website scraping, custom datasets). Multi-channel with signal-based plays. Deep CRM rebuild or Salesforce integration. Typical for recruitment, M&A advisory, multi-country campaigns.

Includes Growth plus: Custom Clay sourcing pipelines, RB2B website visitor identification, multi-country sequences, Salesforce integration, weekly strategy calls.

Standalone Clay builds (waterfall, Claygent, CRM sync, signal scoring) run separately from EUR 2K to EUR 6K. See the Clay agency page for scope.

03Always included

What every engagement
ships with.

No matter which tier, every closed:in engagement starts with the same baseline. The differences above are about depth and channels, not about whether the foundation is solid.

Clay enrichment

Clay is the orchestration layer of every engagement. Lead sourcing across 120+ providers, waterfall email finding, signal capture and Claygent personalisation. As an official Clay Solutions Partner we run Clay daily for our own outbound and for every client.

Full sending infrastructure

Alternate sending domains, inbox configuration, SPF/DKIM/DMARC records, 14-21 day warmup and continuous deliverability monitoring. Your primary domain stays clean for transactional and customer communication.

Multi-step sequence and copy

3 to 5 step sequences written for your ICP and tone. Iterated based on reply data. Plain text by default, A/B tested across subject lines, openers and CTAs.

Weekly reporting and reply handling

Every Sunday you get a structured weekly report: replies, qualified opportunities, pipeline value and copy iterations. Replies are routed into your CRM with full context so your sales team picks up where the conversation left off.

06Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

How much does closed:in cost?
closed:in outbound engagements run between EUR 4,000 and EUR 8,000 per month. Where you land in that range depends on four drivers: ICP complexity (standard vs custom-sourced data via Clay), sending volume (number of inboxes and domains), channels (cold email only vs multi-channel with LinkedIn) and CRM depth (light reporting vs full pipeline orchestration in Close, HubSpot or Salesforce). Most engagements run on a 3 month standard term.
What is included in the monthly retainer?
Every engagement includes the same foundation: full sending infrastructure (domains, inboxes, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, deliverability monitoring), Clay-based lead enrichment, multi-step cold email sequences, list building and ICP scoring, weekly reporting, and reply handling wired into your CRM. LinkedIn outreach and signal-based plays are added on top of that base depending on the plan.
Is there a setup fee?
Yes. There is a one-time setup fee at kickoff covering domain purchase, inbox configuration, 14-21 day warmup, Clay table build and copy framework. The setup fee plus the first month are paid together at kickoff. Month 2 and Month 3 are billed at the start of each month.
Why does Clay drive a lot of the pricing?
Clay is the enrichment and orchestration layer that connects everything: lead sourcing across 120+ providers, waterfall email finding, signal capture, Claygent personalisation and CRM sync. The more custom the ICP (think hospitality, boutique retail, M&A advisory, niche industrials), the more Clay engineering goes into building the table that actually finds those prospects. Standard ICPs that are clean in Apollo sit at the lower end of the range. Custom Clay workflows with multi-source enrichment and Claygent prompts sit higher.
Why is pricing a range and not a fixed number?
Two clients on paper sending the same volume can require very different work. A SaaS client with a clean Apollo ICP and HubSpot already wired up is a different build than a recruitment firm targeting PE-backed portfolio CFOs across 4 countries with custom Clay sourcing and a Close CRM rebuild. We quote after a 30 minute discovery call when we have seen the ICP, the channels you want, and the CRM you run.
What is the minimum engagement length?
Standard engagements run for 3 months. That is the realistic timeline to set up infrastructure, warm inboxes, build the Clay table, launch sequences, see initial reply data and optimize. Most clients see their first qualified meeting within 2-3 weeks of launch, with engagements often extending into month 4 onward as performance compounds.
Do you offer Clay-only or consulting engagements?
Yes. Standalone Clay builds (waterfall setup, Claygent prompts, CRM sync, signal scoring) run between EUR 2K and EUR 6K depending on scope. GTM engineering consulting for in-house teams is scoped per project. Book a discovery call to discuss.
Do you offer performance-based pricing?
No. We run on a fixed monthly retainer because outbound results compound on top of stable infrastructure and Clay tables. Performance-only pricing creates pressure to chase short-term reply rate spikes at the cost of long-term deliverability and pipeline quality, which is the opposite of what we are paid for.
