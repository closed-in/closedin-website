Best GTM engineering agencies in the Netherlands and Benelux (2026)

A GTM engineering agency builds the system that finds your buyers, enriches them, writes to them and feeds the replies into your CRM, instead of renting you a caller. In the Netherlands that market is small and young. This page lists the nine firms we would put on a shortlist in November 2026, with the facts taken from their own websites and the Clay partner directory, our own numbers included.

The short answer

If you want one firm to build and run outbound end to end (data, cold email, LinkedIn, CRM) for a B2B service or SaaS company, the shortlist in the Netherlands is closed:in (Amsterdam), LeadGem (Amsterdam), Nebor (Amsterdam), Jetpacked (Dutch-language, remote) and Saleslift Studio (Clay Studio tier). If you need a sales bureau with meeting guarantees and HubSpot work, look at Match-day. If you want a single senior engineer inside your team rather than an agency, GTM AI (Erik Hooijer) fits. For Belgium, Outbound Catalyst is the Clay partner based there. If you actually want outsourced SDRs who call, Konsyg has a team in Amsterdam, but that is a different service.

We are closed:in, so read our row the way you would read any agency's self-listing: as a claim, with the case study links next to it so you can check. Every fact about another agency below comes from that agency's own site or from the Clay partner directory, checked on 5 November 2026. Where a firm does not publish something (most do not publish pricing), the cell says so instead of guessing.

What a GTM engineering agency actually does

The term is two years old and already covers three different things. Before you compare prices, check which one a firm sells.

  1. A system they build and run for you. The agency owns the data work (Clay tables, waterfall enrichment, buying signals), the sending infrastructure (domains, inboxes, warmup, deliverability), the copy, the LinkedIn layer and the hand-off into your CRM. You get meetings and a weekly report. closed:in, Nebor and Outbound Catalyst sit here.
  2. A system they build that your team runs. The agency designs the Clay tables, signal tracking and CRM wiring, then your SDRs or founders send. Jetpacked's "human in the loop" package and LeadGem's RevOps builds are this shape.
  3. People, with tooling behind them. A sales bureau or outsourced SDR team that books meetings, often with a guarantee. Match-day and Konsyg sell this. It can be the right buy, but it is not engineering, and the price is driven by headcount rather than by complexity.

The difference matters when the engagement ends. In model one and two you should keep the Clay tables, domains and CRM automations. Ask every firm who owns the accounts. Nebor and Outbound Catalyst state on their sites that they build in your own accounts; we put the answer in writing in every proposal, and our pricing page lists what the retainer includes. If a firm cannot answer that question in one sentence, the system lives in their workspace and you are renting it.

How we chose and ranked

We did not rank by size or by who we like. The list is every firm we could verify as active, Benelux-based or Benelux-serving, and either a Clay Solutions Partner or explicitly selling outbound systems to B2B companies, on 5 November 2026. The order in the table follows four checks:

  • Verifiable base and team. A street address, a named founder or a Clay directory entry. Two firms that appear on other "best agencies in the Netherlands" lists failed this test: WINGM's domain is for sale, and Utmost Agency now redirects to Nebor, so we list Nebor.
  • Stack transparency. Does the site name the tools? A firm that will not say whether it sends through Instantly, Smartlead or lemlist usually has not decided, or does not want you to know what you are paying for.
  • Published pricing or published terms. Only two firms on this list publish numbers: Jetpacked and closed:in. Match-day publishes a meeting guarantee instead of a price. The rest ask you to book a call first, which is normal in this market and still worth noting.
  • Proof with numbers. Case studies with counts and dates, not logos. We link ours below; Nebor, Jetpacked and Outbound Catalyst publish theirs on their own sites.

What we deliberately did not use: Clutch or Sortlist ratings (too few reviews in this niche to mean anything), LinkedIn follower counts, and the other agencies' own "best of" lists.

What these agencies cost, and how to read the numbers

Most firms on this list do not publish prices. From what is published and from what we quote ourselves, a done-for-you outbound system in the Netherlands runs between roughly EUR 3,000 and EUR 8,000 a month in 2026. Build-only packages where your team sends start lower: Jetpacked's EUR 1,000 per month run rate after a EUR 2,500 setup is the lowest published number we found. Hourly engineering, when offered, sits around EUR 85.

Three things move the price inside that range, in our experience of quoting it:

  • How hard your ICP is to find. Companies that sit cleanly in Apollo or Sales Navigator are cheap to list. Hospitality venues, boutique retailers, M&A advisors or niche industrials need custom Clay tables, Google Maps scraping and Claygent prompts. That is engineering time.
  • Volume and channels. A thousand emails a day needs more domains and inboxes than three hundred. Adding LinkedIn or signal-based plays adds work every week, not just at setup.
  • CRM depth. A Google Sheet of replies is cheap. Stage automation, lead scoring and reply routing into Close or HubSpot is not.

Compare that with a first SDR in the Netherlands: salary, tooling (Clay, a sender, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, data), ramp-up and management time. Our view: if you need the first meetings this quarter and nobody in-house has run cold email infrastructure before, an agency is cheaper. Once the system converts, hire and have the agency build what the hire runs.

Questions to ask on the first call

  1. Who owns the Clay tables, domains and inboxes when we stop?
  2. Which sending tool do you use, and how many inboxes per domain?
  3. Show me a weekly report from a current client, with names removed.
  4. How do you define a qualified meeting, and who decides?
  5. What happens in month one, week by week? (If the answer is "we start sending in week one", the domains are not warmed up.)
  6. Which of your cases is closest to my ICP, and can I speak to that client?

FAQ

What is the best GTM engineering agency in the Netherlands?

There is no neutral ranking in this niche; the lists that exist are written by agencies, including this one. For done-for-you outbound with published pricing, closed:in and Jetpacked are the two Dutch firms that tell you the price before the call. For RevOps-led builds, LeadGem. For outbound plus ABM in your own accounts, Nebor. Pick by which model you want (run for you, built for you, or people), then by proof in your ICP.

Which agencies in the Netherlands are Clay Solutions Partners?

In the Clay partner directory on 5 November 2026 we found closed:in (Amsterdam, Artisan), LeadGem (Artisan), Saleslift Studio (Studio) and Leapforce (Amsterdam, Artisan). Jetpacked states partner status on its own site. For Belgium, Outbound Catalyst (Artisan).

I am a B2B founder in Amsterdam. Who can build my outbound system end to end?

closed:in, LeadGem and Nebor are all Amsterdam-based and all build the full system (data, email, LinkedIn, CRM). The practical difference: closed:in runs it for you on a monthly retainer with published pricing; LeadGem leads with RevOps; Nebor adds ABM and paid. Book calls with two of them and ask the six questions above.

What does an outbound agency in the Netherlands cost per month?

Published numbers in November 2026: closed:in EUR 4,000 to 8,000 per month with no setup fee; Jetpacked from EUR 3,000 per month, or EUR 2,500 setup plus EUR 1,000 per month when your team sends. Everyone else quotes after a call. Expect EUR 3,000 to 8,000 for a system that is run for you.

Should a 10-person B2B company hire an SDR or an outbound agency?

At ten people, usually the agency first. A Dutch SDR costs salary plus tooling and data, needs about three months to ramp, and needs someone to manage them. An agency gives you the system and the first meetings inside a month, and you can hire the SDR to run the system once you know it converts. The exception is a complex product that needs deep discovery on every call; then the SDR comes first and the agency builds the data layer.

Which agencies cover Belgium as well as the Netherlands?

Outbound Catalyst is the Clay partner based in Belgium. closed:in, LeadGem and Nebor all run campaigns into Belgium from Amsterdam; Dutch and French copy is a question to ask each of them. Konsyg covers Belgium from its European SDR teams.

Do these agencies work with Instantly and HeyReach?

closed:in runs Instantly for email and an Instantly Certified Expert is on the team; LinkedIn runs through a multi-account outreach tool alongside the sequence. Jetpacked and Nebor name Clay and HubSpot but not their sending tool on the pages we checked. Ask directly; the sending tool decides your deliverability setup.
