04Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Can a European agency run outbound into the US market?
Yes. closed:in runs outbound from Amsterdam into the US market for European B2B companies expanding to the US and for US-based B2B teams that want a senior outbound partner without US agency pricing. Our Amsterdam morning overlaps with US East Coast pre-market hours, our copy is in native English, and our infrastructure handles US time zone send windows automatically.
What kind of US-targeting companies do you work with?
B2B SaaS, professional services, agencies and product companies with ACVs of USD 5K+ selling into US mid-market. European founders building their first US pipeline and US-based teams that want a Clay-native, signal-based outbound system.
How is US cold email different from European outbound?
US buyers tolerate more direct value framing, more pattern interrupts and higher-volume sequences than UK or EU buyers. Sales cycles are faster, especially in tech. closed:in tunes copy, sequencing and send windows for US conventions while keeping deliverability and ICP precision intact.
Is cold email legal in the US?
Yes, under the CAN-SPAM Act. B2B cold email is allowed with clear sender identification, a valid physical address, accurate subject lines and a working unsubscribe mechanism. closed:in builds compliant campaigns by default and only targets verified business email addresses.
Do you handle US time zones?
Yes. Send windows are configured per US time zone (ET, CT, MT, PT). Reporting is delivered in time slots that work for both sides. Our Amsterdam morning overlaps with US East Coast 5-11am, which captures the prime cold email open window.
How much does outbound for US-targeting companies cost?
Standard engagements run USD 3.5K to USD 9K per month plus a one-time setup fee, depending on volume, sequencing and CRM complexity. Engagements are 3 months minimum.
