What's inside the playbook

The infrastructure checklist,
broken into 6 chunks.

01

Sending domains

How to pick alternate domains, structure subdomains, set up MX records and avoid the domain pitfalls that kill deliverability before you send a single email.

02

Inboxes & warmup

The exact warmup schedule we use to take a fresh inbox from 0 to 50 emails/day, plus which warmup tool actually works and which ones leak signals.

03

SPF / DKIM / DMARC

Plain-language explanation of all three records, which values to set for outbound on Google Workspace and Microsoft 365, and how to test that they actually pass.

04

Lead list hygiene

The Clay waterfall we use to verify business emails, the bounce-rate threshold that gets you to the spam folder, and how to scrub before you send.

05

Sequence timing

Day-by-day spacing between touches, when to switch from email to LinkedIn, and the cutoff point where follow-ups stop helping and start hurting reply rates.

06

Reply handling

How to wire replies into Close, HubSpot or Salesforce automatically, the 3 reply categories that matter, and the auto-tag rules we use to keep the SDR inbox clean.
