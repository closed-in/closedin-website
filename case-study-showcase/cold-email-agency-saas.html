01SaaS outbound is different

Generic agencies treat
SaaS like a list size.

B2B SaaS sales cycles are faster than enterprise services. The buyer is often technical. The ICP is narrower than most agencies assume. Copy that works for staffing companies will get ignored by SaaS founders. closed:in builds outbound systems tuned specifically for SaaS sales motion: tight ICP segmentation, product-aware copy, integration-led narratives, and demo booking flows that respect the buyer's time.

04Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Why does B2B SaaS need a specialist cold email agency?
SaaS outbound is different from generic B2B outbound. The sales cycle is faster, the buyer is often technical, the ICP is usually narrow, and the bar for relevance is high. A SaaS-specialist agency understands product-market fit signals, integration plays, competitor displacement narratives and how to write copy that respects a technical buyer.
What kind of SaaS companies do you work with?
Mostly B2B SaaS with ACVs of EUR 5K to EUR 50K, selling into mid-market. Common verticals: vertical SaaS, sales tech, fintech, HR tech, hospitality tech and AI/ML tooling. We have documented results from clients like Jamezz (hospitality SaaS) and Rewize (AI inventory forecasting for e-commerce).
How do you find buyers who are not in standard databases?
For niche SaaS ICPs we build custom data sourcing pipelines: Google Maps scraping for local business owners, LinkedIn Sales Navigator with multi-signal filters, Clay Claygent prompts that parse company websites for ICP fit indicators, and signal stacking across funding events, tech stack changes and hiring patterns.
How is cold email for SaaS priced?
Standard engagements run EUR 3K to EUR 8K per month plus a one-time setup fee. Engagements are 3 months minimum. Most SaaS clients see ROI within month 2 if their ACV is EUR 8K or above.
How fast do SaaS clients see qualified demos?
Infrastructure setup takes 2-3 weeks. First demos are typically booked in week 4-5 after launch. Documented case studies: Jamezz booked 81 qualified leads in 60 days, Mistertafel produced 96 retail opportunities in 30 days.
Do you integrate with our CRM and demo booking tool?
Yes. We wire replies and bookings into Close, HubSpot, Salesforce or Pipedrive, and connect Calendly, Chili Piper, Neetocal or your existing demo scheduler so qualified replies turn into booked demos automatically.
